## 8. DOCUMENT MODEL AND NEWLINE POLICY

The document must remain plain text.

A logical newline is a real paragraph break.

A visual wrap caused by the viewport width is not a stored newline.

Do not insert newline characters merely because a line wraps visually.

Preserve:

- Empty paragraphs.
- Consecutive empty paragraphs.
- Leading newline characters.
- Trailing newline characters.
- Repeated blank lines.

### Canonical newline policy

Normalize incoming text to LF.

Input may contain:

- LF (`\n`)
- CRLF (`\r\n`)
- CR (`\r`)
- Mixed newline sequences.

Normalization order:

1. Convert CRLF to LF.
2. Convert remaining CR to LF.
3. Preserve all resulting logical line breaks.

Do not turn CRLF into two newlines.

Do not collapse repeated blank lines.

Do not discard leading or trailing newlines.

Use `GtkTextBuffer` insertion APIs for normalized text.

When exporting text, retrieve the plain text from the buffer and serialize using LF.

The buffer must remain the authoritative document representation.

### Required invariant

Loading or pasting supported text and then exporting it must preserve the sequence of logical characters and paragraph breaks, subject only to the documented newline normalization policy.
